## What it is

A browser game. You get clues about a Premier League footballer and you guess who it is. Built for fun, shipped on Vercel.

## What I built

The full game loop: clue display, guess input, score tracking, and a new round. Deployed so anyone can play from a link.

## What I would do differently

Add difficulty levels and a shareable result. Right now every round feels the same, and the game ends without a reason to replay.
